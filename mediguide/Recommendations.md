# Special Considerations for Healthcare Software
### 1. Liability Concerns
All open source licenses include disclaimer of warranty and liability, but for healthcare:

Add explicit medical disclaimers in your app
Include liability waiver in terms of service
Consider separate liability insurance
License doesn't provide liability protection (you need separate legal measures)

### 2. Medical Data Privacy
The license doesn't handle data privacy:

You still need HIPAA/GDPR compliance
License covers code, not how data is handled
Implement privacy controls separately
Document data handling practices

### 3. Regulatory Approval
Some jurisdictions require medical device registration:

Open source doesn't exempt from regulation
AGPL-3.0 aids transparency for regulatory review
Consider FDA regulations if deploying in US
Consult healthcare lawyer for your region

### 4. Medical Accuracy & Liability
License doesn't guarantee quality:

Include disclaimers about AI limitations
Require medical advisor review before deployment
Document validation and testing processes
Clear user warnings about seeking professional care
